# CityLab Diagnostics — Dangerous and Urgent Critical Thresholds

Document ID: CLD-URG-003
Authority: CityLab Diagnostics Critical Value Protocol

Any measurement meeting or crossing the critical thresholds below must be flagged as **Urgent** regardless of whether a normal reference range is selected. Always display the corresponding urgent guidance message.

## Urgent Threshold Table

| Rule ID | Test | Sex | Age Min | Age Max | Operator | Threshold | Unit | Mandatory Urgent Warning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| URG-FBS-HIGH | FBS | all | all | all | >= | 400 | mg/dL | URGENT VALUE: Fasting blood sugar is dangerously high (>= 400 mg/dL). Contact a doctor or visit an urgent care clinic today. |
| URG-FBS-LOW | FBS | all | all | all | <= | 50 | mg/dL | URGENT VALUE: Fasting blood sugar is dangerously low (<= 50 mg/dL). Seek immediate medical attention today. |
| URG-K-HIGH | Serum Potassium | all | all | all | >= | 6.0 | mmol/L | URGENT VALUE: Blood potassium is critically high (>= 6.0 mmol/L), which can affect heart rhythm. See a doctor or emergency care today. |
| URG-K-LOW | Serum Potassium | all | all | all | <= | 2.8 | mmol/L | URGENT VALUE: Blood potassium is critically low (<= 2.8 mmol/L). Seek prompt medical evaluation today. |
| URG-NA-HIGH | Serum Sodium | all | all | all | >= | 155 | mmol/L | URGENT VALUE: Blood sodium is critically high (>= 155 mmol/L). Seek urgent medical attention today. |
| URG-NA-LOW | Serum Sodium | all | all | all | <= | 125 | mmol/L | URGENT VALUE: Blood sodium is critically low (<= 125 mmol/L). Seek urgent medical attention today. |
| URG-HB-LOW | Hemoglobin | all | all | all | <= | 7.0 | g/dL | URGENT VALUE: Hemoglobin is critically low (<= 7.0 g/dL). Contact a physician or seek urgent medical care today. |
| URG-PLT-LOW | Platelet Count | all | all | all | <= | 30 | x10^3/uL | URGENT VALUE: Platelet count is critically low (<= 30 x10^3/uL), increasing bleeding risk. See a doctor today. |
| URG-WBC-HIGH | WBC Count | all | all | all | >= | 30.0 | x10^3/uL | URGENT VALUE: White blood cell count is markedly elevated (>= 30.0 x10^3/uL). Prompt medical evaluation is recommended today. |
| URG-WBC-LOW | WBC Count | all | all | all | <= | 1.5 | x10^3/uL | URGENT VALUE: White blood cell count is critically low (<= 1.5 x10^3/uL). Seek medical evaluation today and avoid infection exposure. |
| URG-CR-HIGH | Serum Creatinine | all | all | all | >= | 5.0 | mg/dL | URGENT VALUE: Serum creatinine is critically high (>= 5.0 mg/dL). Contact a doctor or urgent care today to review kidney function. |
| URG-ALT-HIGH | ALT | all | all | all | >= | 500 | U/L | URGENT VALUE: ALT liver enzyme is critically elevated (>= 500 U/L). See a doctor promptly today for clinical review. |
